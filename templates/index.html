GAME FEATURES

EVERY DETAIL IS A CLUE

🔦

FIRST-PERSON EXPLORATION

Full pointer-lock FPS movement. Walk through atmospheric 3D crime scenes with head-bob, spatial audio, and real-time flashlight illumination.

🔮

UV BLACKLIGHT MODE

Toggle UV [F] to reveal fluorescent evidence invisible in white light — hidden codes, fingerprints, floor inscriptions, and blood trails.

🧠

AI SUSPECT INTERROGATION

Question Dr. Aris Thorne, Maya Lin, and Victor Vance. Powered by Gemini AI with stress-tracking — corner them with evidence and watch them crack.

🧩

5 PUZZLE TYPES

Keypad, combination lock, color sequence, cipher wheel, pressure plates, mirror alignment. Each room has its own puzzle mechanic.

🌐

REAL-TIME MULTIPLAYER

Invite investigators via PeerJS peer-to-peer networking. See other players as wireframe avatars. Share evidence and solve together.

🗺️

EVIDENCE MIND MAP

Live SVG node-graph connects clues to suspects. Workbench combines inventory items for bonus discoveries and hidden case files.
