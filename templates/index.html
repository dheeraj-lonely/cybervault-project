CYBERVAULT // DIGITAL FORENSICS EDUCATION

Build practical forensic investigation skills in a safe, guided learning environment.

CyberVault helps students learn how digital evidence is collected, analyzed, and documented without exposing them to live systems, private data, or unsafe forensic workflows. The platform focuses on understanding the process behind modern investigations.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Digital forensics is difficult to teach without realistic practice.

Beginners often learn the theory of evidence handling, but they rarely get the chance to experience the real investigation process. Complex tools, large data sets, strict procedures, and chain-of-custody rules make practical forensic learning difficult to deliver in a safe environment.

OUR SOLUTION

CyberVault creates a controlled forensic learning experience.

Students work with fictional case scenarios, simulated evidence, timestamps, messages, browser activity, and access records to reconstruct timelines, identify patterns, and prepare investigation reports. The process is realistic, educational, and ethically safe.
